# Lessons Learned - Week 08 Reflection

## 1. What planning activity helped most in monitoring progress?
For me, the **Risk Register (Week 06)** was the most valuable tool during this monitoring phase. When we realized the model training was moving too slowly (Risk R1), we didn't have to scramble for a solution. Because we had already identified "Cloud GPUs" as a mitigation strategy and budgeted for it in the Cost Plan, I could immediately authorize the switch to paid resources without needing a long approval meeting. It turned a potential crisis into a simple administrative task.

## 2. What would you do differently in future projects?
I would implement **shorter check-in cycles** during the "Data Collection" phase. I assumed the "Filter low-quality images" task was going smoothly, but Qasdina was actually struggling with the dataset quality for days before raising a flag. In the future, I would ask for a "Data Quality Sample" report after the first 24 hours rather than waiting for the task deadline to ask for a status update.

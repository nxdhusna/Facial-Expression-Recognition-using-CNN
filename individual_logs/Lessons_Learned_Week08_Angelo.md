# Lessons Learned - Week 08 Reflection

## 1. What planning activity helped most in monitoring progress?
The **WBS (Week 04)** was the most helpful tool. Because we broke down "Model Training" into specific sub-tasks (4.3.1, 4.3.2), we could spot exactly where we got stuck (the compute phase) rather than just thinking "Phase 4 is slow." The **Cost Plan (Week 05)** was also critical because it gave us the confidence to approve the "Paid GPU" corrective action immediately, knowing we had exactly RM 120 set aside for it.

## 2. What would you do differently in future projects?
In future AI projects, I would schedule a **"Hardware Test Run"** in Week 1 rather than Week 4. We waited until the actual training phase to test our GPU capabilities, which is why we hit the delay (Risk R1). Testing the infrastructure early would have allowed us to switch to the Cloud before the critical path began.

# Lessons Learned - Week 08 Reflection

## 1. What planning activity helped most in monitoring progress?
The **Network Diagram / Dependency Mapping** in our Project Schedule was the most helpful. It made me realize that my task (2.3.1 Filter Images) was on the "Critical Path." Seeing visually that the entire "Model Design" phase was blocked until I finished cleaning the data motivated me to put in extra hours. Without that clear dependency view, I might have treated the deadline as flexible, not realizing it would delay the whole project.

## 2. What would you do differently in future projects?
I would allocate a specific buffer time for **"Data Auditing"** before starting the actual cleaning. I underestimated how many images in the FER-2013 dataset would be mislabeled or corrupt. In the future, I would download a small 5% sample first to estimate the error rate, then calculate the cleaning time based on that, rather than assuming a standard "2 days" for 10,000 images.
